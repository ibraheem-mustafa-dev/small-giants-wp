## Shared causes proven so far

- **Colours that resolve to nothing paint cocoa.** The pages ask for `custom-mm-*` colours (242 references in 11 posts); the palette pushed after the 2026-10-31 re-extraction (`b5d9e14f2`) names them `mm-*`. An unresolved colour falls back to `currentColor`, the cocoa text colour, so every pink and yellow band is a solid cocoa block with its cocoa text invisible (home, shop, gifts, help and contact heroes; gift-card band; FAQ open row; delivery row; review cards).
- **Section padding inside header and footer rows.** `theme/sgs-theme/assets/css/layout-tokens.css` gives page-section padding (64/20 mobile, 112/40 desktop) to every `sgs/container` not nested in another section-padded one; a header or footer row is not one, so the header bar is 216px at 375 and 284px at 1440 (draft 64/84), and the account and basket icons stack.
- **Sideways overflow at 375:** the footer's Instagram gallery (`-20px` side margins, 415px wide) on every page; home also overflows from its gifts carousel and review row; gifts at 768 from a section container.
- **Static copies, not working blocks:** the single-product template holds no buy box, gallery or add-to-cart (74 containers, 17 buttons, 5 option pickers); home's reviews, gifts cards and FAQ are containers; the 2026-10-28 home holds no `sgs/product-card` (the `sourceMode` cause in the brief belongs to the later clone).
- **Capture config:** the shop and product surfaces captured the home page (`build/qa/parity/{shop,product}.fill-base.mjs` live URL was `/`); corrected to `/shop/` and `/product/zookies/`.

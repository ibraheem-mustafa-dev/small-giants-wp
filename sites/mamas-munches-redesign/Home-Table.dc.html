Our story

A story that started with a friend

My story with lactation cookies started when my friend started struggling with breastfeeding her newborn.

At the time, we had no choice but to start making lactation cookies ourselves, praying that it would work for the sake of her baby and amazingly, it did! These cookies helped her milk supply almost immediately and I was so grateful to have been able to make her early motherhood days just a bit easier.

Now I hope that I can do the same thing for you. Using natural, wholesome ingredients, everything is made with care and intention for breastfeeding mums and their new beautiful babies.

Zainab
Founder, Mama’s Munches

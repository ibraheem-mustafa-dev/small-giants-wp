# MIC website redesign: design brief

For the design agent drawing the new Muslims In Construction (MIC) website in Claude Design. The project's `CLAUDE.md` and `WHAT-WE-BUILD.md` (uploaded with this brief) say how to build and what can be built. This brief says what the site is for, who it serves, what each page holds and how it should feel.

Facts come from MIC's current site (`muslimsinconstruction.uk`, read 2026-11-28). Where this brief says "ask MIC", leave a clearly marked placeholder in the draft. Never invent a name, a quote, a number or a logo.

## 1. Who MIC is

Muslims in Construction is a UK professional network that connects, supports and uplifts Muslims working across construction and the built environment: site staff, engineers, managers, directors, students and people starting out. It is volunteer-run and guided by Islamic values. Membership is free, and non-Muslims who share its values are welcome. Members are mostly in the UK, with more in the UAE and across the Muslim world.

What MIC does:
- **Events across the UK.** The flagship is the annual gala, whose 2026 edition is on 28 November 2026 at the Saffron Gallery in London E15, with about 450 guests, the MIC Awards, keynotes, dinner and networking. Every ticket release sold out. MIC also runs regional events: MIC Connect in Birmingham, a Manchester Sunday Social, "From Foundations to Skylines", "Levelling Up Birmingham" and a MIC Sisters arts social. The first in-person event, at London South Bank University in April 2025, drew 200 people and sold out.
- **Mentorship.** Members are matched with approved mentors, and experienced people sign up to mentor.
- **Advocacy and representation** in the industry.
- **A directory, being built,** of industry experts, trustworthy tradespeople and aspiring professionals.
- **A WhatsApp community** and active LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
- **Merchandise:** a polo shirt (£15.00), a T-shirt (£12.99) and a cap (£9.99).

The current site's own line is "Building Excellence With Faith As Our Foundation". The hadith on the team page captures the spirit: "Verily, the believers are like bricks of a building, each part strengthening the other" (Sahih Muslim 2585).

## 2. What the site must do, in priority order

1. **Turn a visitor into a member.** The visitor joins the WhatsApp community and the mailing list, and becomes a member. This is the main action on every page.
2. **Sell event tickets, and show what MIC has already done.** Selling out every event is MIC's strongest proof, so make it visible.
3. **Win sponsors and partners.** Organisations need a clear reason to sponsor, what they get, and one easy way to ask. The impact report download belongs here.
4. **Recruit mentors, mentees and volunteers.**
5. **Sell merchandise.**

## 3. Who visits, and what each needs

| Visitor | Arrives from | Needs to see quickly |
|---|---|---|
| A construction professional or student, often on a phone | A WhatsApp share, Instagram, TikTok or LinkedIn | What MIC is, the next event, how to join |
| A gala or event guest | The event link or a printed QR code | Date, venue, tickets, what is included, how to sign in on the night |
| A sponsor or partner, usually a company director or marketing lead | LinkedIn, or a referral from another sponsor | Reach and audience, past sponsors, what sponsorship includes, a contact |
| A would-be mentor or volunteer | An event, a post | What it involves, how much time it takes, how to sign up |

Most visitors arrive on a phone from social media, so design the phone layout first.

## 4. Pages

- **Home.** Lead with who MIC is and the main action (join). Then: the next event with a countdown, and a sold-out label when it sells out; the four pillars (Building the Ummah, Barakah Connections, Deen and Development, Uplifting Muslim Builders, with their current wording tightened); proof (numbers, past events, partner logos); mentorship; merchandise (three products); a sponsor call; join.
- **About.** The story and mission, the timeline already on the current site (launched January 2024, then the survey, networking events, the mentorship programme, advocacy, community outreach, the first in-person event in April 2025, Birmingham MIC Connect in August 2025, and the galas), an impact report download, and a link to the team.
- **Team.** The three co-founders with photo, role and short biography (current `/team` page: Abdullah Shah, Chair; Ishaaq Mohamed; Zeshan Wassim, Vice-Chair), then the wider team of regional leads, operations and ambassadors, each with a LinkedIn link. End with a volunteer call.
- **Events.** Upcoming events first, then past events with a photo, a place and one line each.
- **Event page** (one template for every event). Date, time, venue with a map, dress code, parking, what the ticket includes, ticket tiers with prices and availability (Early Bird, Standard, Final Release, each able to show sold out), the agenda, speakers, awards and nominations where there are any, sponsors of that event, and "Add to calendar". Tickets are bought through the site's shop basket and checkout. Draw each ticket tier as a choice the guest picks, then attendee details, then payment.
- **Gala 2026, after the night.** The same event page in its "after" state: thank you, the award winners, a photo gallery, the sponsors thanked, and an invitation to register interest for the 2027 gala. This state matters from 29 November.
- **Mentorship.** Two clear routes, for mentees and for mentors. Each says what it involves and how to sign up. The current page sends mentors to an outside mentoring platform where they "enter MIC as your community"; ask MIC whether that platform stays.
- **Sponsors and partners.** Why sponsor (the audience and reach, in numbers from MIC), sponsorship options (ask MIC for the packages; if there are none, use an enquiry form), the logo strip of past sponsors and partners, the impact report download and an enquiry form.
- **Get involved.** One page gathering join (free membership, WhatsApp, the mailing list), volunteer (with a form), mentor, speak at an event and partner.
- **Directory: coming soon.** A short page that explains the directory being built and collects interest (name, email, trade or role). Do not draw a working directory yet.
- **Shop, product page, basket, checkout, my account.** Clothing and headwear today. Products show sizes where they have them, and the shop must also hold event tickets.
- **Contact.** A form (name, email, a reason: volunteering, careers, partnering, sponsorship, tickets, other; then the message), the email `info@muslimsinconstruction.uk`, the social links and the FAQ already on the current contact page.
- **Legal pages:** privacy, terms and conditions, refunds and returns. Their text exists and is kept as it is.

Header: logo, Events, About, Mentorship, Sponsors, Shop, Contact, a basket icon and a "Join MIC" button. Footer: the mission line, the menus, the social links (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok), a mailing-list sign-up, the legal links and the line "Website by Small Giants Studio".

## 5. Look and feel

- **Feeling:** professional and proud, warm and faith-rooted. Think of a respected industry body that is also a community. It should not feel like a mosque noticeboard, and not like a corporate contractor.
- **Colours:** start from the logo: deep green `#025652` and gold `#d4a73c`, with white and a warm off-white. Gold is an accent for awards, highlights and key buttons, never for body text on white (it fails contrast). The design agent may widen the palette, keeping these two as the identity.
- **Logo:** the MIC symbol in full green (on the current site, `wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MIC-Symbol-Full-Green-500px`). Ask MIC for the full lockup and a white version.
- **Pattern:** a quiet Islamic geometric pattern (for example an eight-point star grid) works as a section background or divider, used sparingly.
- **Type:** the design agent's choice. It needs a strong, confident heading face and a very readable body face.
- **Imagery:** real MIC event photos and real members on site and in offices (ask MIC for the gala, London South Bank and Birmingham photos). Where none exist yet, use marked placeholders, not stock photos of hard hats.
- **Language:** plain UK English. Islamic terms are used naturally, as MIC already does (ummah, barakah, deen, Insha'Allah), without overdoing it. No emoji in headings or body copy; the current site leans on them heavily and the new one should not.

## 6. What to drop from the current site

- The placeholder testimonials ("Lorem ipsum", "Gerald Clark, Company Name"), and the "Projects" page, a theme demo with fake projects and the phone number `+123-456-7890`.
- "Meet the Founders (WEAK)" as a heading.
- The countdown showing all zeros after the event.
- Every page redirecting to the gala page.
- Contact forms that show "There was an error trying to submit your form": every form on the new site must send.

## 7. Content to ask MIC for

Real testimonials from attendees and sponsors (name, role, company, permission); audience numbers (members, WhatsApp size, event attendance, social following); past sponsors' and partners' logos with permission; sponsorship packages and prices; the impact report PDF; gala 2026 award categories, winners and photos; event photos; the full logo set; whether the outside mentoring platform stays; a mailing list provider, if they have one.

## 8. Decided for this draft

- Tickets are sold through the site's own shop, basket and checkout, as products with tiers, not through a separate events plugin. This keeps one checkout for tickets and merchandise.
- The gala's "after" state is designed now, since the gala happens before the site launches.
- The directory is a register-interest page until MIC has the data and a way to check listings.

# Delivery options

> Titles and descriptions for the three shipping methods in the shop: tracked delivery, free delivery over £75, and collection in Birmingham.

## Method 1: tracked delivery

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| **Method title** | Tracked delivery |
| **Short description (checkout)** | Tracked to your door anywhere in the UK. In-stock frames go out within one working day. |
| **Cost** | £3.95 |

Longer note, for the delivery page: Orders go by tracked UK courier, with a tracking link by email. Frames in stock leave within one working day. Prescription orders are made first, which takes around 7 to 10 working days, and then they are sent. Delivery is to UK addresses only.

## Method 2: free delivery

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| **Method title** | Free delivery over £75 |
| **Short description (checkout)** | Free tracked delivery because your order is over £75. Same dispatch times as standard. |
| **Cost** | Free (orders of £75 and over) |

Note: the title carries the threshold so the customer sees why it is free. The threshold is measured on the basket total after discounts, frames and lenses included. If the basket is under £75 this method must not show; the cart's free-delivery line already tells the customer how much more to add.

## Method 3: collection

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| **Method title** | Collect in Birmingham |
| **Short description (checkout)** | Free. We'll message you when your order is ready, and fit the frames to your face while you wait. |
| **Cost** | Free |

Longer note, for the delivery page: Collect from 644 Washwood Heath Rd, Birmingham B8 2HQ. We will message you when your order is ready, so please wait for that before you come, and bring your order number. We adjust the frames to fit you on the spot, at no charge. No appointment is needed during opening hours.

## Optional extras

**Short line for the checkout page above the methods:**
Frames in stock go out within one working day. Prescription lenses take around 7 to 10 working days to make.

**Short line for an order with prescription lenses (if a prescription item is in the basket):**
These lenses are made for you, so please allow 7 to 10 working days before dispatch or collection.

## Not covered, on purpose

- **International delivery.** Delivery is to UK addresses only.
- **Express or next-day delivery.** Not offered, so it isn't described.
- **Returns postage.** This belongs in the Terms and the returns FAQ, not in a shipping-method field.

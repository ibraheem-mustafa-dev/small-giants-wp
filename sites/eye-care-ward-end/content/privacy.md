## Health information is special

An eye test result and a prescription are **health data**. UK data protection law calls this "special category data" and asks for extra care. I treat it as confidential, I only use it for the purposes below, and I never use it for marketing.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me to:

1. **See** the personal information I hold about you, and get a copy.
2. **Correct** anything that is wrong or incomplete.
3. **Delete** your information. This has limits: I can't delete records I'm legally or professionally required to keep, such as accounts and clinical records, until their retention period ends.
4. **Restrict** how I use it, in some situations.
5. **Object** to uses based on legitimate interests, and to marketing at any time.
6. **Move** the information you gave me to you or another provider, where I hold it by consent or contract.
7. **Withdraw consent** at any time, where consent is the basis. This doesn't affect what I did before you withdrew.

To use any of these, email [CONFIRM: privacy contact email] or write to the address above. I'll reply within one month, and there's normally no charge. I may need to check who you are first, because I don't want to hand your health information to the wrong person.

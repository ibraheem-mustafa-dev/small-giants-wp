# Privacy policy

> How Ward End Eye Care looks after your personal information, under UK data protection law.

**Last updated:** 7 October 2026

## Who we are

Ward End Eye Care is an independent optician at 644 Washwood Heath Rd, Birmingham B8 2HQ. Our optician, Fatima Nawaz, is registered with the General Optical Council (GOC).

We are the "data controller" for the personal information described here. That means we decide why and how it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it. This policy covers this website, online orders, and the records we hold from eye tests and appointments at the practice.

To ask us anything about your data, use the contact form on this site or write to us at the address above.

## What we collect

**When you order or contact us**
- Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery and billing addresses.
- What you ordered, the price, and the delivery method you chose.
- Messages you send us by email, the contact form or WhatsApp.
- Payment confirmation. Your card details go straight to the payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

**Prescription and eye-health information**
- If you order prescription lenses, we collect your prescription (and your pupillary distance if you give it), the date of the eye test, and who issued it. You can type it in, upload a photo, or send it after you order.
- If you have an eye test with us, we keep a clinical record: your history, symptoms, test results, anything we find, our advice and any referral.

**About your use of the website**
- Basic technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages you visit, collected through cookies and similar tools.

## Health information is special

An eye test result and a prescription are **health data**. UK data protection law calls this "special category data" and asks for extra care. We treat it as confidential, we only use it for the purposes below, and we never use it for marketing.

## Why we use your information, and our legal basis

| What we do | Information used | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Take, process and deliver your order, and take payment | Contact, address, order, payment confirmation | Contract: it is needed to fulfil your order |
| Make your lenses to your prescription | Prescription, frame details | Contract, plus your **explicit consent** to use the health information in your prescription (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). You give this when you submit it with your order. You can withdraw it, though we then can't make the lenses |
| Carry out an eye test and keep a clinical record | Health information | Providing health care: the data is used by a registered professional bound by a duty of confidentiality (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h)), with contract or legitimate interests as the ordinary basis under Article 6 |
| Answer your questions and give after-sales support | Contact details, messages | Legitimate interests (answering you), or contract where it relates to an order |
| Keep accounting and tax records | Order and payment records | Legal obligation |
| Keep the website secure and prevent fraud | Technical data, order data | Legitimate interests |
| Understand how the site is used | Cookie and usage data | Consent, where cookies are not strictly necessary |
| Send you news or offers | Name, email | Your consent, or for existing customers the "soft opt-in" for similar products. You can refuse at any time |
| Send an eye-test reminder | Contact details, test date | Health care and legitimate interests |

## Who we share it with

We only share what is needed, and only with organisations that handle it under a written agreement or as a regulated professional.

- **Lens laboratories** that make the lenses. They receive your prescription, the frame measurements and an order reference. They do not need your name or address unless the lab delivers to you directly.
- **Delivery companies** receive your name, address and phone number or email so they can deliver.
- **Our payment provider** processes your payment under its own privacy policy.
- **Website, hosting and email services** that run the shop and send order emails.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)**, if you message us there. Anything you send is handled under WhatsApp's own terms. If you would rather not send a prescription that way, use the upload on the website or email us.
- **Our accountant or bookkeeper**, who sees order and payment records.
- **Other health professionals** (your GP or an eye hospital) if we refer you. We will tell you first, and we will only do this without your agreement if it is urgent and necessary to protect you.
- **Regulators and the law:** the General Optical Council, the ICO, the police or a court, but only where we are legally required or permitted to.

We never sell your data.

**Outside the UK.** Some providers (for example analytics or email tools) may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the UK's approved safeguards, such as an adequacy decision or the ICO's international data transfer agreement.

## How long we keep it

| Information | How long | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Eye test and clinical records (adults) | 10 years from your last visit, in line with College of Optometrists guidance | Professional record-keeping and clinical safety |
| Eye test records for children | Until the child turns 25, or 10 years after the last visit if that is longer | As above |
| Prescription uploaded with an online order | The same period as clinical records, because it is health information | So we can remake or check lenses and deal with any claim |
| Order, invoice and payment records | 6 years from the end of the tax year | Tax and accounting law |
| Website messages and enquiries | 2 years after the last message | To handle follow-up questions |
| Marketing subscriptions | Until you unsubscribe | Your consent |
| Cookie and analytics data | Up to 14 months | Site improvement |

When the time is up we delete or anonymise the information securely.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

1. **See** the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy.
2. **Correct** anything that is wrong or incomplete.
3. **Delete** your information. This has limits: we can't delete records we are legally or professionally required to keep, such as accounts and clinical records, until their retention period ends.
4. **Restrict** how we use it, in some situations.
5. **Object** to uses based on legitimate interests, and to marketing at any time.
6. **Move** the information you gave us to you or another provider, where we hold it by consent or contract.
7. **Withdraw consent** at any time, where consent is the basis. This doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew.

To use any of these, use the contact form on this site or write to the address above. We will reply within one month, and there is normally no charge. We may need to check who you are first, because we don't want to hand your health information to the wrong person.

## Complaints

We would like the chance to put things right, so please tell us first. If you are still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator:

- Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
- Helpline: 0303 123 1113

Because our optician is GOC-registered, you can also raise concerns about professional conduct with the General Optical Council at optical.org.

## Security

Order information is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to the website's admin and to clinical records is limited to the people who need it and protected by passwords and, where available, two-step verification.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at real risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

## Children

Eye tests for children are done with a parent or guardian involved. We don't knowingly collect information from under-16s through the website without a parent or guardian. Glasses for under-16s are supplied in person, as explained in our Terms.

## Cookies

We use cookies that are strictly necessary to run the shop (your cart, checkout and security), and these need no consent. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie banner or your browser settings, and you can block cookies in your browser, though the cart and checkout may then not work.

**What you need.** To have lenses made, you need a valid prescription from a UK optician or ophthalmologist, issued within the last two years. We can't make lenses without one, and an online order is not an eye test.

**How to give it to us.** Type it in at checkout, upload a photo, or send it after you order. If your pupillary distance (PD) isn't on it, we can measure it from a photo or in person.

**Checking.** We check every prescription before lenses are made. If something looks unusual, unclear or inconsistent, we will contact you before we go ahead. Your order may be paused until we hear from you.

**Your responsibility.** You are responsible for giving us the prescription exactly as it was written. If the lenses match the prescription you gave us and you then find the prescription was wrong, we are not responsible for that, though we will always try to help you work out what to do next.

**Under-16s and registered sight-impaired customers.** The law (Opticians Act 1989, section 27) says glasses can only be supplied to children under 16, or to people registered as sight-impaired or severely sight-impaired, under the supervision of a registered optician. If you are buying for either, please contact us before you order. We will arrange the sale, the measuring, the fitting and the collection with Fatima in person.

**Contact lenses** are not sold on this site.

**An eye test matters.** If your vision has changed, or you have pain, flashes, floaters or sudden loss of sight, don't wait for glasses. Get your eyes examined, and urgently if the symptoms are sudden.

3. Return them unworn and in the condition you received them, with the case, cloth, tags and warranty card.
4. We will refund the price and the standard delivery cost you paid within 14 days of getting the goods back, or sooner if you send proof of postage. You pay the cost of sending them back unless they are faulty or we sent the wrong item.

**Our 30-day promise.** We give you longer than the law requires: you can return unworn frames within **30 days** of receiving them for a full refund. The 30 days start on the day your parcel is delivered or you collect your order.

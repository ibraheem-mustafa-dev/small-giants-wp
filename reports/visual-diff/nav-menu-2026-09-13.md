`.claude/reports/2026-09-12-nav-menu-visual-review-register.md` — `itemSmartContrast`
defaulted ON and silently overrode an operator's explicit `itemColourHover` with a computed
WCAG-safe colour whenever it failed contrast against the resolved hover background. That is
the real explanation behind a "my colour isn't applying" report that turned out not to be a
rendering bug.

## What changed

New `megaDrawerFallbackIds` attribute + `MegaDrawerPanel.js` inspector panel (Settings tab).
A mega-typed menu item authored with real nested child links (a `core/navigation-submenu`
pointing at an `sgs_mega_menu` CPT, with its own menu children — not the CPT panel content)
renders those children as an ordinary accordion in the DRAWER when its identifier is listed
in `megaDrawerFallbackIds`, instead of the item's existing default: a plain-link degrade.
Bar/desktop rendering is untouched in every case (`sgs_nav_menu_render_items()` was not
touched). Files: `plugins/sgs-blocks/src/blocks/nav-menu/block.json`, `render.php`,
`edit.js`, `useNavMenuSource.js`, `utils.js`, new `MegaDrawerPanel.js`;
`plugins/sgs-blocks/includes/nav-menu-markup.php` (`sgs_nav_menu_render_items_drawer()`).

## Why intent_capture, not first_paint

There is no "before" render to diff against for the ON state — it is a brand-new opt-in
branch with no prior equivalent. The real question is "does the current state match what
was intended", checked directly against the live canary.

## Assertions

1. A mega item with **no** nested children, `megaDrawerFallbackIds` unset (default `[]`):
   drawer renders the item as a plain link — byte-identical to pre-existing behaviour.
2. A mega item **with** nested children, `megaDrawerFallbackIds` unset: drawer STILL renders
   a plain link (having children alone must not change anything — the toggle must be
   explicit).
3. The same mega item, its identifier now listed in `megaDrawerFallbackIds`: drawer renders
   it as an ordinary accordion (`<details>`, its nested child as a real sub-link), not the
   plain-link degrade.
4. In all three cases the desktop/bar form renders the full mega-panel disclosure,
   unaffected.

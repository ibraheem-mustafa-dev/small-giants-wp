## What changed

negation from the `section.sgs-hero` rule (`calc(var(--wp--style--root--padding-left/right,
0px) * -1)`), keeping `width: auto` and `max-width: none`. Root cause + qc-council
validation of the fix-shape (including a falsified first proposal) is in the commit
message.

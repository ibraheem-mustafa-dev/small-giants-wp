## What changed

Added a per-item `decorative` field (boolean, default `false`) to the `items[]` repeater's
item schema, scoped to the `image-badge` variant only — `icon-circle`/`text-only` variants
have no uploaded image to gate. `edit.js`'s badge-item editor shows a `ToggleControl` next
to the `MediaPicker`, gated to `'image-badge' === badgeStyle`; `render.php` blanks that
badge's alt and adds `aria-hidden="true"` on its `<img>` when set.

## Assertion

An unset item renders byte-identical (`decorative` defaults false per item). Marking one
badge decorative in a multi-badge bar affects only that item, and the control is invisible
for the two non-image variants (nothing for a client to misuse).

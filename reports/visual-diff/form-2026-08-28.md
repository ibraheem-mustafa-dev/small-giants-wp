## Why intent-capture, not before/after

No before-state diff is obtainable or meaningful here. The defect being fixed was that
`--third` rendered IDENTICALLY to `--half`, so a before/after pixel diff of a third-width
field against a half-width field is by definition a null image — the bug was that they
looked the same. The assertion below is therefore stated first and measured against the
live DOM, which is the sanctioned intent-capture shape.

Form CSS is lifted into `uploads/sgs-css/`, so grepping page HTML proves nothing. Every
number below is `getComputedStyle` / `getBoundingClientRect` on the real painted element.

## Assertion

After the six-track change, on a container >= 560px:
  1. `.sgs-form__inner` computes to SIX grid tracks, not two.
  2. `--third` computes `grid-column: span 2` and occupies ~1/3 of the container.
  3. `--half`  computes `grid-column: span 3` and occupies ~1/2 of the container.
  4. Three thirds share ONE row; two halves share ONE row.

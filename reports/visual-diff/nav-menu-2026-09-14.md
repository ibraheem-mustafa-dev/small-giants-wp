## What changed

`plugins/sgs-blocks/src/shared/nav-interactivity/mega-disclosure.js` +
`plugins/sgs-blocks/src/blocks/nav-menu/style.css` — a page-content-embedded
`sgs/nav-menu`'s open dropdown/mega panel now reparents to `<body>` on open
(`reparentPanelIfNeeded()`), freezing at its already-computed screen position via
`--sgs-mm-fixed-top/-left` and switching to `position:fixed` through the new
`[data-sgs-nav-fixed]` CSS rule, escaping `sgs/container`'s load-bearing child-lift
stacking context that previously capped it below the sticky header regardless of its
own z-index (`P-NAV-DROPDOWN-STACKING-IN-PAGE-CONTENT`). The panel reverts to its
original DOM position on every close path (self-close, ESC, Tab-out, single-open,
bfcache restore, and a new scroll/resize close) via the existing `watchOpenState`
funnel. Header-placed instances are excluded by construction
(`needsStackingFix()` — no `.sgs-site-header` ancestor) and never reparent.

## Assertions (stated before measuring)

1. On a page-content-embedded `sgs/nav-menu` instance, opening a dropdown moves its
   panel to be a child of `<body>`, `position:fixed`, with a z-index greater than
   the sticky header's `z-index:100`.
2. At the panel's own rendered screen coordinates, `document.elementFromPoint()`
   returns the panel itself (or a descendant) — nothing, including the header,
   paints over it.
3. `.entry-content` (the ancestor whose child-lift rule caused the original cap)
   still measures `position:relative;z-index:1` — confirming the root cause is
   present and untouched, and the fix works by escaping it rather than removing it.
4. Closing the panel (any path) reverts it to its original DOM position, with
   `position:absolute` and no `[data-sgs-nav-fixed]` marker.
5. Scrolling/resizing the viewport while the panel is open closes it.
6. The HEADER's own `sgs/nav-menu` instance is unaffected: its dropdown never
   reparents, stays `position:absolute`, and open/close/ESC/ArrowDown-keyboard-open
   all behave exactly as before.

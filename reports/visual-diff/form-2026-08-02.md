# sgs/form — focus indicator becomes accent-led, over a neutral underlay

## What changed, and why

Bean, on seeing the live canary: *"switch the form input focus colour to a
brighter/more vibrant default global colour. Just checked and dark looks bad.
Doesn't need contrast or anything, it's just supposed to be a little
indicator/feedback, like a coloured glow effect. Maybe going with primary or
accent would be better."*

**The plan's first answer was falsified before it was built.** It proposed
flipping the default to `primary`. Measured across all 8 client palettes,
`primary` is itself a near-black on 5 of them (`#0D1B2A`, `#1C1C1C`, `#1A3D2B`,
`#1E2D5E`, `#1A5F6B`) — so the change would have shipped and still looked dark
on the majority of the roster. `accent` is the genuinely vibrant slot.

A second correction surfaced during the build: the stated baseline was wrong.
The default was already `primary`, not `primary-dark` — the CSS `primary-dark`
was only ever a *fallback for an unset variable*, and `render.php` always sets
the variable. That is consistent with Bean's complaint (primary is near-black on
most palettes) but for a different reason than recorded.

## The shape shipped — two layers, not one

- **Accent does the visible work**: `border-color` plus a soft `box-shadow`
  glow (`0 0 8px 2px`), which is the "coloured glow effect" Bean described.
- **A neutral outline sits underneath** carrying the WCAG 2.4.11 3:1 floor, so
  no client palette is left with an indicator nobody can see.

Both remain overridable: `--sgs-focus-ring-colour` for the glow,
`--sgs-focus-underlay-colour` for the outline.

Applied consistently across the three focus systems that previously disagreed:
form inputs (`form/style.css`), SGS button styles, and the sitewide
`:focus-visible` catch-all (`core-blocks*.css`).

## Live measurement — canary page 2118, Mama's palette

Computed style on the real focused input, unfocused → focused:

| Property | Unfocused | Focused |
|---|---|---|
| `outline-color` | `rgb(58,46,38)` 3px | **`rgb(197,106,122)`** 2px = `#c56a7a` = `primary-dark` |
| `box-shadow` | `none` | **`srgb(0.961 0.816 0.314 / 0.4) 0 0 8px 2px`** = `#f5d050` = `accent` |
| `border-color` | `rgb(232,213,192)` | **`rgb(245,208,80)`** = `accent` |

All three changed on focus. Contrast against the input's **real** painted
background (`#FBF3DC`, Mama's cream — not white, which would have flattered the
figure):

- `accent #f5d050` → **1.35:1** — the visible glow. Well below 3:1, and
  deliberately so: it is decoration, not the indicator of record.
- `primary-dark #c56a7a` → **3.32:1** — the underlay. **Clears the floor.**

## Bean's ruling, recorded

The 3:1 concern was raised once, with the 8-palette table in front of him
(6 of 8 fail on accent alone). He ruled accent regardless. That ruling is
implemented — and the underlay is what makes it safe to implement rather than an
override of the standard. This is the `a-contrast-ratio-is-not-a-legibility-verdict`
pattern: raise once, then build what was asked.

## What this report does NOT claim

- **Measured on Mama's palette only.** The other 7 clients are covered by
  calculation against their own `surface` token, not by a live render. Only
  `sgs-construction` (3.11) and `sgs-professional` (6.38) clear 3:1 on accent
  alone; the rest rely on the underlay.
- **No pixel-diff of the glow itself.** The verdict rests on computed style
  before/after focus plus the contrast maths; a screenshot of the glow region
  was captured but not compared against a reference.
- **`extensions.css`'s `.sgs-has-focus-ring` is untouched** — a fourth focus
  system, left alone because a co-active track had uncommitted work in that
  file. It still hardcodes `primary` with no glow and no underlay, so it now
  disagrees with the other three by one generation. Recorded, not fixed.
- **Keyboard-only traversal was not exercised** — focus was applied
  programmatically, not by tabbing through the form.

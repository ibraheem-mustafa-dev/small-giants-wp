# Skeleton stage: declarations decide what a part is

**Why (Bean, 2026-10-29):** the skeleton proposal (`scripts/computed-route/lib/skeleton-propose.mjs`, `skeleton-classify.mjs`, `skeleton-rules.mjs`, `data/skeleton-decisions.json`) guesses what each draft part is from content and 65 hand rules, and overrides what the handoff declares. Evidence (`.claude/reports/2026-10-29-skeleton-reevaluation.md`, `.claude/reports/2026-10-29-surface-fate.txt`, `.claude/reports/2026-10-29-recognition-gaps.md`): Indus's and Eye Care's WhatsApp buttons are declared `global` surfaces (surfaces.json S-15 and S-07, README "Global layout", the markup) yet the footer rule absorbs them and the button rule makes them `sgs/button`; Eye Care's size-guide `modal`, product-tabs `panel` and three of four mega-menu panels become containers or grids; Mama's checkout `form-step`s are unwrapped by the hero rule; Eye Care's `sgs-brand-marquee` section was guessed from its typed names (which stand for brand logos, Eye Care register S9). Only Eye Care's pre-contract draft carries `sgs-` names; Indus and Mama's follow the contract, whose prompt says "Don't map the design to WordPress or SGS blocks". The framework DB's naming columns (`block_attributes.derived_selector`, `equivalent_implementations`) are generated from templates, not read from the blocks (1,027 of 2,670 selectors exist in source; 506 rows are core blocks with invalid classes), and the route reads none of them.

## Rulings (Bean, 2026-10-29)

1. **Declared surfaces decide.** A surfaces.json record's `kind` and `name` decide its block family (a `global` fixed button becomes its floating block; a `modal` becomes `sgs/modal`; a set of `panel`s the tabs block; a mega-menu panel `sgs/mega-panel`; a `form-step` a form step). Content rules work only inside that choice and never override it; a rule that would is reported as a conflict. The Spec 48 lines that forbid names or absorb declared surfaces (FR-48-6 "never a name", the footer rows' "its surface is absorbed") change.
2. **Section identity is declared in surfaces.json `blocks`** with a fixed purpose vocabulary we own (FR-48A-6: schemas are ours): every page and part lists its sections in order, each `{ purpose, root }` (the purpose from the list, the section root's `data-dc-tpl` id). The purpose decides the block family through the framework DB; the code supplies structure, content and values (Spec 48A §1 stays: the code is the primary source of everything but identity). The README stays the human guide (FR-48A-8 unchanged); the prompt still never maps the design to SGS blocks; `handoff-check.mjs` refuses an unknown purpose or a root that is no element. Until a client re-exports, its free-text `blocks` entries (Indus 28 of 61 records, Mama's 10 of 58) are read as evidence matched to the code by order.
3. **The DB's naming columns are regenerated from the blocks' real source** (render.php, style.css, `block.json` `supports.sgs.elements`): only classes that exist are recorded, core blocks get none, so the columns become a trustworthy name-to-block-and-element map.
4. **Floating UI by mechanism, per element:** only an element the draft fixes to the viewport is a self-floating block's floating variant (`sgs/whatsapp-cta`, `sgs/google-rating-badge`) placed in the footer post as on eye-care-test; the same blocks in flow stay where the draft puts them; back-to-top and the reading-progress bar are Spec 18 Customiser options; persistent bottom bars are missing-features rows; a sticky notice bar goes to the header's top row like the trust bar. With ruling 1 the declared `global` surface carries the identity.
5. Typed brand names stand for brand logos (Eye Care register S9): a brand marquee is `sgs/brand-strip` reading product brands with logos.

## Steps

1. **Purpose vocabulary** (`.claude/claude-design-handoff/handoff-schema/section-purposes.json`, the schema's enum source): drafted from the framework's block families (DB `blocks` titles and descriptions, `supports.sgs`), the three clients' sections (Eye Care's 42 names, Indus's and Mama's free-text lists, the README Pages sections) and WooCommerce's pieces; each purpose names the block family it maps to and the DB facts that confirm it. Bean confirms the list.
2. **Contract:** `surfaces.schema.json` `blocks` items become `{ purpose (enum), root, note? }`, required on page and part records; `PROMPT.txt`, the handoff `CLAUDE.md` and `METHOD.md` tell Claude Design to fill it (purposes, never block slugs); Spec 48A FR-48A-7 and §3 readers; `handoff-check.mjs` validates it. Bean re-uploads the schema folder to Claude Design.
3. **Skeleton stage:** a declarations reader joins each surface record and each declared section to its code root; the classifier takes the declared kind and purpose as the deciding evidence and the existing rules act only within it; conflicts are reported. The hand rules that only identify a block retire (each named in the commit). The held classifier work (`fix/recognition` `efd6a5d39`, `12989ddff`; the filter-drawer rule in `fix/w3-debug` `c7dacb937`) is reconciled into this or dropped.
4. **DB naming columns** regenerated from source by the seeder (`sgs-update-v2.py` and its helpers `assign-canonical.py`, `enrich-db.py`), then a reseed (with the gallery `mediaItems` and `sgs/whatsapp-cta` `variant` changes already on `main`).
5. **Proof against the goal** on the three clients: every declared surface and section becomes the block its kind or purpose names (WhatsApp floating, size guide modal, product tabs, mega panels, brand marquee as logos, checkout steps), from the current exports with their free-text lists, and from a re-exported draft with the vocabulary.
6. Then the full council over the whole fix pass, fresh intakes, Bean's review, the third re-clone.

Built in at least three commits per step that touches a shared mechanism (FP-5).

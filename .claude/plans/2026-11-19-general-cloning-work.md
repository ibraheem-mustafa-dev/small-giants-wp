## Bean's rulings that bind this plan

2026-11-18:
- Fix what exists before building on it.
- A site is cloned as a foundation built once (tokens with the animation records, media, menus, business details, then header, footer, nav drawer and basket drawer), then one surface at a time, each fix made in the system.
- A clone goes through by default. Bean reads no long review table; the agent compares the built clone with the draft, proposes fixes and says where Bean's own eye would do better. Bean looks at one page per layout type.
- A button that matches a preset takes the preset; one that differs keeps its difference. A preset takes the size most of its family share.
- A draft carries the tokens and presets it uses, as many as it has and under its own names, set on the blocks they apply to.
- What no setting holds is written as Custom CSS by the route, never by hand.
- The handoff gets no Components page, no block map and no section-level block choice. It gets a list of what we have, in design words.
- A change for one client never alters what another client's site renders unless that site opts in. (Narrowed 2026-11-20: this covers optional settings that extend a feature; a fix applies everywhere.)

2026-11-20:
- The opt-in rule covers only optional settings that can be seen as extensions to features; fixes apply everywhere (plugin round 4's form fixes land for every site).
- The fault rounds cured symptoms one at a time. Go to the root cause: treat each fault as an instance of a mechanism, fix the mechanism, and guard against every instance of it, so the same kind of fault stops reappearing in other places.
- Four pages are held out: `indus-foods/about`, `indus-foods/food-service`, `eye-care-ward-end/about` and `eye-care-ward-end/contact` stay out of every detector list, sweep and helper brief and are cloned once at the end with no fixing afterwards, because the 16 filled surfaces are the ones the fixes were tuned on (a side reviewer's note, accepted).

2026-11-21:
- A look a draft element paints is read by a block or the proposal fails (read-or-fail). The fix also records a look the host block has no part for (`no-part`) without failing the proposal by default; such rows are never counted as conserved, and a strict switch makes them fail. Bean decides when strict becomes the default (open; recommended: after the plugin round adds the missing settings).
- One combined re-clone with two-panel pictures at the end of the mechanisms, and the full route suite once per landing batch (approved with the session plan).

2026-11-19:
- The Claude Design instructions trust the tool's judgement and list no cases: tidy the drafts, say what changed, ask only where a change would alter the design or the meaning is unclear.
- A button is cloned as a preset when the draft mounts it with a preset name, and with its own styles otherwise. No rule forces a differing button to be a named version.
- Bean does not re-export a project to test the instructions: the handoff files are edited here.
- Stale intake records are fixed by rerunning intake, not worked around.
- "Control" means an editor sidebar control. A button or field on a page is called a button or a field.
- A clone is judged beside the draft only: pictures are two panels, draft and clone. The hand-finished site is not a comparison panel, because it carries intentional differences from the draft.
- The two new sites are SGS (Small Giants Studio, no shop) and MIC (the redesign with a shop). Nothing is created on the hosting account for them until Bean says so.
- A draft's labelled empty picture slots are cloned as tiles showing the label (the slot's box and name), never an invented picture and never the design tool's placeholder icon; the picture replaces the name when supplied, and the report lists the pictures to supply (Bean chose this over literal placeholder icons and over hiding the strip).

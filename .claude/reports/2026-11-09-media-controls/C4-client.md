# C4 Client-in-the-editor review of Spec 49 v0.5

Reviewer view: a non-technical owner, and the support person taking their call at 11pm. Read: the full spec, the v0.3 and v0.4 diffs (stat only), T5, T6 and N for how others label these controls. Rulings (media-first, separate sizing settings that combine, Circle in the Shape grid) are not reversed; risks inside them are flagged.

## STRENGTHS

- Media-first with no box or frame vocabulary (§3, §6.3) matches how an owner thinks: "the photo", not "the container".
- Controls that do nothing are omitted (§9.3) and, where a visible field loses to another, it says why ("Not used while Width and Height are both set", §9.2). This fixes N's finding that Min height silently overwrote desktop.
- "Mobile: using Tablet" with a grey placeholder, dots on tabs and one-step undo (§9.1, §9.2) answers "why does it look like this on my phone".
- Circle drawn from the client's own photo in the Shape grid, auto-setting 1:1 (§7.5, §9.2 ShapeField), and Replace warning that Alt describes the old photo (§9.2 MediaSlotField).
- Plain Fit captions (Crop to fill, Show all) and "Cover image before play" follow what T6 shows is the clearest wording in other tools.
- Empty state with four plain buttons, and an editor message for a deleted file (§9.5), where N saw a silent broken image.

## MUST-FIX

1. Two different things are called "fill". Height has "Fill" (§7.2, §9.2 Sizing fields) and Fit has "Crop to fill" (§9.2 FitField); Fit's `fill` is called "Stretch". A client told "make the photo fill the card" will pick Height Fill expecting a cropped photo, or Stretch expecting it to fill. Fix: rename Height's choice to "Match its space" with help "Makes the photo as tall as the space around it, such as a card stretched by the text beside it." Keep "Crop to fill" for Fit only. Update §8 Height ("labelled Fill"), §9.3, the walkthrough and the cloner table label.
2. Height Fill with no height in the layout silently becomes 3:2 (§7.2, FR-49-38). The client sees a 3:2 photo, cannot tell why and cannot change it (Ratio is hidden while Fill is on, §9.3). Fix: when Fill resolves with no parent height, show under the field "This layout gives the photo no height, so it is shown at 3:2. Pick a Height instead, or put the photo in a card with a height." and offer a "Use a Height" button. Also tell the client when Fill makes the parent a grid: the container emitter changing its layout (§7.2, last Fill rule) needs a line in the parent's panel, "Set to a one-column grid so the photo can fill it."
3. Overlay strength defaults to 100 (§8 `OverlayOpacity`). A client who picks black to darken a hero gets a solid black box and thinks the photo is broken. Fix: the first time an Overlay colour is chosen, write strength 40 as part of the same undo step, and label the slider "Overlay strength" with help "0 shows only the photo, 100 hides it." The same change goes into §9.3's "Strength after a colour is set" row.
4. Fit and Focal vanish when the box does not crop (§9.3 rule table, §8 defaults). A client who wants "move the crop on the phone" looks for a focal control, finds none, and nothing says a shape is needed first. They also appear and disappear as Ratio is changed (choosing 3:2 on a 3:2 photo removes them). Fix: when Fit and Focal are omitted, show one line at their place in Size and crop: "To choose which part of the photo shows, first give it a shape: pick an Aspect ratio or a Height." Show it on every device where nothing crops.
5. Height alone crops the photo to a full-width strip (§7.2 rows "Height only" and "Width auto with a Height keeps proportions"). A client who types Height 300 to make a photo smaller gets a cropped banner; they would need to know to set Width to Auto. Fix: with Height set and Width and Ratio empty, show under Height: "The photo is full width and cropped to this height." with a button "Keep the photo's shape" (sets Width to Auto).
6. There is no plain statement of why the picture looks as it does (§9.2 Sizing fields, §7.2). Seven size fields plus Fit and Focal combine by rules only the spec knows. Fix: at the top of Size and crop, a live one-line summary resolved at the active device, for example "Mobile: full width, square (1:1), photo cropped to fill, centred on the focal point" or "Mobile: 300 tall, as wide as the photo allows". Add it to §9.4 and AC-12.
7. Picking Circle gives an oval when a Ratio or both Width and Height are already set (§7.5 sets 1:1 only when no Ratio is set). Fix: when Circle is chosen and the box resolves non-square at the device, show "This is an oval because the photo area is not square." with a button "Make it square (1:1)". Never silently leave an oval under the name Circle.
8. A mask shape removes Border with no explanation (§7.5: Border omitted; only Corners and Shadow get a note). Fix: when a mask shape is on, show in Border and shape "Borders are not available on this shape. Choose Circle, Ellipse or Pill to keep a border."
9. Panel Reset on a non-desktop device is ambiguous (§9.1: "Every panel has Reset in its header menu"). A client on Mobile pressing Reset may wipe Desktop. Fix: off desktop it reads "Reset Mobile in this panel" and affects only that device; add "Reset all devices in this panel" as a second item with undo. Also fix the field Reset: it reads "Reset to Tablet" off desktop (§9.2) even when Tablet holds nothing and Mobile really inherits Desktop; it must name the true source, as the placeholder does ("Reset to Desktop").
10. Limits cannot be cancelled on a smaller device. §7.2 says "auto or natural cancels an inherited one", but §8 gives Min and Max fields only a length, so a Desktop Max height of 300px cannot be removed on Mobile except by typing a huge number. Fix: every Limits field offers "None" that writes the cancel value, and the placeholder reads "None".

## SHOULD-FIX

- Defaults and help for Width, Height, Ratio, Fit and Focal together. Nothing set should read as: Aspect ratio chip "Photo's own" selected; Width placeholder "Photo's own size"; Height placeholder "Photo's own size"; Limits closed; Fit and Focal replaced by the line in MUST-FIX 4. Help text under the group: "Width and Height set the size. Aspect ratio sets the shape. If you set all three, Width and Height win."
- Rename Fit (§9.2 FitField, §9.4): the label "Fit" means nothing to this user. Use "How the photo sits in its shape". Chips: Crop to fill, Show all (primary); Stretch, Actual size, Never enlarge under "More" (five choices, two of which distort or shrink the photo, are too many for the main view). Stretch gets help "Can squash the photo."
- Rename Limits (§9.2, §9.4) to "Smallest and largest size", with fields "Never narrower than", "Never wider than", "Never shorter than", "Never taller than". Collapsed by default, as specified.
- Focal label and help (§9.2 FocalField): label "Part of the photo to keep in view"; keep the specified help line. Add a one-line example under the picker: "Drag the dot onto the face or product."
- Replace behaviour (§9.2 MediaSlotField): "Keep focal point" is a tick on a flow the client has already completed. Show it in the panel after Replace as an action: "Focal point was reset to the centre. [Put it back]" (one undo step). The toolbar Replace on a non-desktop device must say which device it changes: "Replacing the photo on Mobile only" (the spec does not say, §9.4 toolbar).
- Different photo on a phone (§9.2 MediaSlotField, FR-49-8): add an explicit button on Tablet and Mobile, "Use a different photo on Mobile", and a "Use the Desktop photo again" in place of Reset. Without it the client replaces the wrong device's photo.
- Hover zoom and lift duplicate wording (§8 `ShadowLiftOnHover` in Shadow, §10.1 Hover "Lift"). Two controls both called lift on hover. Keep one, in Hover, and let the Shadow panel link to it; the Hover subtitle in §9.4 already points the other way.
- Hover zoom needs an amount and a preview (§10.1 names Zoom with no setting). Chips Subtle (5%), Medium (10%), Strong (20%), and a note "Hover shows on the live page, not in the editor" if the editor cannot preview it; §9.7 does not say.
- Parallax wording (§10.2, §9.6). "Add" in the motion line (§9.6) suggests it adds something but "Add creates nothing". Label the button "Choose motion". Direction chips read "With the page" and "Against the page", not a signed ratio. Pinned reads "Stay still while the page scrolls". Add under Parallax: "Not shown to visitors who have reduced motion turned on in their device" and, if the editor does not animate it, "Parallax shows on the live page only".
- Video poster and Autoplay (§9.3 Poster, Playback). With Autoplay on the cover image shows only while the video loads. Help under Cover image: "Shown until the video starts. With Autoplay on, visitors see it only while it loads." The greyed Muted and Inline need "Browsers only autoplay muted videos."
- Video and embed Alt label "Title (for screen readers)" (§9.3). Use "Describe the video for someone who cannot see it". For embeds, prefill from the video's own title where available, otherwise "Video", so the required Title is not a stopper; keep the warning.
- Embed ratios (§9.3, §9.2): offering 1:1, 9:16 on an embed with no Fit gives black bars inside the player that the client cannot remove (§7.9 Fit not offered for embeds). Offer 16:9, 4:3, 21:9 and 9:16 only, with help "The video keeps its own shape inside; other shapes add black bars."
- Embed errors (§9.5): "This address is not a supported video" should say what works: "Paste a YouTube or Vimeo web address. Other sites are not supported." Embed "Start at" gets "(seconds)".
- Hidden controls keep stale values (§9.3, FR-49-21). When Fit or Focal reappears after being hidden, show a one-time "Kept from earlier" tag so a returning value is not a surprise.
- Jargon labels in §9.4: Padding becomes "Space inside the border"; Blend becomes "Mix with what is behind" (options Normal, Darken, Lighten, Multiply); Greyscale becomes "Black and white"; Decorative becomes "Just decoration (screen readers skip it)"; Captions file becomes "Subtitles file (.vtt)"; Background colour on a "Show all" photo becomes "Colour behind the photo".
- Photo opacity beside Overlay (§9.4 Overlay and filters): a client wanting a darker photo may lower Photo opacity and lighten it on a white page. Help: "Photo opacity makes the photo see-through. To darken it, use Overlay colour."

## MISSING

- No plain-English summary of the resolved size and crop at a device (MUST-FIX 6).
- No "Advanced" fold. Order (-20 to 20), Blend, Limits, Repeat, Tile size and Shadow Custom are too much for a first view; group them under one collapsed "More options" in each panel (§9.4). Thirteen panels in all (§9.4) is the main overload; Shadow, Overlay and filters, and Border and shape should open collapsed with their current value in the header (for example "Shadow: Soft").
- No statement of which device the WordPress preview toggle puts the client on, or a visible "You are editing: Mobile" bar inside the media panels (§9.2 ResponsiveOverride has dots, not a persistent banner). Add "Editing Mobile" at the top of each panel when not on Desktop.
- No editor behaviour specified for hover and parallax preview (§9.7 covers size, video and embed only).
- No cap on how the client sees Circle's relation to Border: Circle, Ellipse and Pill keep borders (§7.5) but nothing tells the client Corners is removed by Circle; add "Corners are not used with this shape".
- No help text anywhere for Alignment and Order beyond names (§9.4 "Position in the layout"); give "Where the photo sits in its row: left, centre or right" and "Order in the row: lower numbers come first".
- Poster and per-device video: no way to say "show the cover image only on phones" (§7.9 per-device sources sibling). Acceptable to defer; record as parked.

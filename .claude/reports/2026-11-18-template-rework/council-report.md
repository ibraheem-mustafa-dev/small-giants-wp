# Council report on the foundation design draft (2026-11-18)

Bean's rulings after this report are in `.claude/plans/2026-11-18-template-rework.md` and override the report's "improved plan" where they differ (no Components page, no section-level block choice, no block map).

Four reviewers (Fable, high effort, blind to each other) read the draft `foundation-design-draft.md` against the Claude Design handoff folder, Specs 48, 48A, 32, 11, 02, 27, 36, 37, 38 and 38A and the three clients' handoff folders. A fifth reader covered the Spec 48 sections, Specs 36 and 37 and `METHOD.md` that the four did not finish. Four fact-checkers (Sonnet, high effort) opened the evidence for 48 findings. This report holds what survived, written for the session that acts on it. Raw outputs: `E:\sgs-tmp\template-rework\council\`.

## Verdict

Change the approach. The draft's four parts should not be built as written. Most of its "foundation" is already Spec 48's stage 2 and header-first slice, largely built; a per-element block map breaks standing rules and would not fix two of the three faults it was aimed at; and the real root cause sits on the design side: no draft defines a button, field or card once, so there is no single definition to read. The cheap, high-yield work is in the Claude Design instructions before Bean designs, in a handful of route and extractor fixes that use what the handoff already says, and in running the route's own process end to end.

## What was confirmed

**The foundation already exists in the spec.** FR-48-18 (stage 2) puts the snapshot, Site Info, media and site build before any tree; FR-48-19 and Appendix B step 20 send the header through first "as a vertical slice, before the other surfaces queue". Not in stage 2: component looks beyond buttons, a drawers-before-pages order, a single command. Spec 48 §8 has Bean asked at stage 1 and next at stage 5, and FR-48-21 needs stage 4 passed "for every surface", so closing each surface by eye needs that text amended. FR-48-22 already holds a kill line (under 60% a surface is hand-built), Status not built.

**The handoff already says much that the route ignores.**
- `surfaces.json` records with `route: "*"` are the shared furniture: 5 on Mama's, 15 on Indus, 18 on Eye Care.
- A record's `kind` already says `accordion` (Mama's S-59 is the Contact FAQ, root 78). No skeleton rule branches on that kind, and no skeleton file names `sgs/accordion`, `sgs/testimonial-slider` or `sgs/tabs`.
- The `blocks` field is free text in three different senses and has no consumer. `data-part` appears in no export and has no reader. No export has been made under the current instructions (the exports' schema copies are 3,969 bytes against the master's 6,158).
- Mama's site-wide entrance is stated three ways (`--mm-reveal-distance` and `--mm-reveal-duration` in `tokens/spacing.css`, records A-04 to A-08, 67 `data-reveal` attributes). Home differs from the other pages (32px and 800ms against 28px and 750ms).
- For looks a state switches on, `animations.json` does not hold the values: 25 of Mama's 28 state records have every `from` and `to` null and 37 of 146 hover records hold an unresolved hole. Those values live in the draft's logic and must be read from code.
- `tokens/` is a summary written after the draft: Mama's drafts contain no `var(--mm-`.

**No draft defines a control once.** The only imported components are large parts (header, footer, product card, forms): 23, 2 and 5 imports. Mama's drafts hold about 130 button elements in about 110 distinct inline looks; her primary yellow appears about 20 times in about 12 size variants. So "one definition per component" and "read the best tick box in the draft" have nothing exact to read. The runtime and intake already support imported components with enum props (FR-48-29: `ProductCard.size`).

**The producer's instructions block Bean's ruling and contradict themselves.** `.claude/claude-design-handoff/CLAUDE.md` says `tokens/` "holds the brand palette, type, spacing and focus only" and excludes the design system's `components/` folder. `scripts/computed-route/lib/handoff.mjs::checkHandoff` refuses any file outside the allowed set, so a `components.json` or a fourth token file fails today. `CLAUDE.md` and `METHOD.md` §10 say never change a draft during a handoff while `PROMPT.txt` says add `data-part` ids and "fix any draft that breaks these". No export has `aria-controls` or `aria-modal`.

**Why Mama's foundation under-delivered.**
- Her snapshot was last extracted on 2026-10-31, before the extractor gained component shadow presets (2026-11-06) and reveal rules (2026-11-07); `7b0290ad1` says a re-extraction is owed. A re-run would still refuse her reveal records: `reveal_tokens.py::_from_values` accepts only `opacity`, `transform` and `filter` (hers use `translate`), `_role` needs a heading or grid word in the selector (hers is `[data-reveal]`), and a refused record leaves no trace. Spec 38A §8.1 has two rule names fitted to Indus.
- The finaliser was run for Mama's on 2026-10-30 (182 picks, `52a53ef61`) and its output was deleted by the 2026-11-02 rewind; `finalise-request.json` holds 190 rows with no picks file. Whether Solve ran cannot be shown (its folder is gitignored). An earlier statement in this session that neither stage ever ran was wrong.
- The audit's two worst faults are confident wrong choices the finaliser never sees: the Contact FAQ rows and Home's `h1` are proposed as `sgs/container` at 0.75 with `low: false`; of 1,618 proposal rows none is an accordion, slider or buy box; `applyPicks` refuses a key outside the request; Solve never swaps a block. So re-running the full process first, as `.claude/prompts/2026-11-18-route-regular-pages.md` asks, cannot change them.
- `site-run.mjs` has six stages and calls neither the finaliser, Solve, the extractor, the snapshot push, media upload nor site build. It runs surfaces in `surfaces.json` order, which puts Mama's header 21st of 24.
- Only three button looks survive extraction (`presets.py::_VARIANT_SLOT`); a fourth is dropped with no gap row. `presetTokens` and `defaultTokens` are read by no route code.
- The extractor lives under `plugins/`, so it is this session's by the boundary, and no task in either plan covers it.

**The button.** "One definition, read everywhere" is already the rule for buttons (Spec 32 FR-32-2, partly built; Spec 11 FR-11-2; Spec 27 FR-30-18) and is unmet. No renderer reads the whole preset: `sgs/button` reads everything but `shadow` and `hover-shadow` (so Mama's hard shadow is stored on each button in her trees); `woocommerce.css` takes eight resting and four hover roles on Proceed, Place order, coupon and mini-cart checkout but takes fill, text and border colour from the palette, fixes a 2px border and ignores the pressed look; `sgs/form`'s button, the basket drawer's buttons and core buttons read little or nothing; `sgs_button_preset_css` accepts only primary and secondary. A fifth named look hits closed lists in about ten places (the block's enum, variations, render and editor lists, the stylesheet, the extractor's three slots, the PHP helper, `fill-preset.mjs`), plus the resolver's enum check, calibration and `wp-build-page.js`. Precedents for rules printed from a snapshot's own keys exist (`theme/sgs-theme/inc/colour-roles.php::colour_roles_css`). A new `{component}Presets` namespace fails `check-preset-token-naming.py` until a block folder of that name exists. WordPress's own `styles.elements` (button, link, textInput, select) is an unused native channel. Applying full presets to WooCommerce's buttons would change Eye Care's border (2px palette to 0px), so each reader needs a per-site switch, as the tick box now has (`4192dcf56`).

**The block map.** A per-element map, and above all the written-out form, should not be built: the hand-made sample for the Contact FAQ is 796 bytes by reference against 2,685 written out, most of it restating code; producer files are "cross-checks, never the source" (Spec 48 §9, FR-48A-7); `data-part` "never" names a block; recognition is "by what the code does, never by names"; and the ban on block names sits in about eight places plus the checker. It would fix G-1 (which one rule over data already present also fixes), half of G-2, and not G-4 (the draft has one element where the block paints two parts). Claude Design's own unprompted block mapping in Mama's README was wrong where it mattered (the gift finder as a choice flow, the announcement strip as a notice banner). What survives: a section-level choice with a fit grade (fits, extend, new), taken from an uploaded catalogue and entered as a declaration that structure checks (the FR-48-31 pattern) into the existing skeleton review rows. The framework database cannot yet feed a catalogue cleanly (296 of 1,814 either-or settings have a description; the matcher returns the nav bar for an FAQ; every block comes to 140 KB), so a catalogue is a cut of the blocks a designer chooses between. `scripts/computed-route/data/capabilities.json` is the existing generated source.

**Special surfaces.** Component definitions would have supplied a quarter to a third of Mama's special-surface Custom CSS (subagent classification, not re-checked); per-part settings are the larger share. The class A swap proved less than the classification claimed: 5 of 11 groups were true misses (2,699 characters removed with identical render on `local-mediatest-classa`); six had no fitting setting, and seven faults in existing settings were found (`class-a-results.md` beside this file).

## The improved plan, ranked by what it changes

1. **Change the Claude Design instructions before Bean designs** (one session; needs Bean's approval of the wording). Controls are components: each repeated control is built once as an imported component with named variants. A Components page mounts every component in every variant and state. Component tokens and the draft's own extras are allowed. `data-part` on sections, widgets and component roots; real ARIA on widgets, drawers and dialogs; the "what the clone reads" rules moved into the standing instructions; the editing contradiction settled; a capability sheet generated from the framework ("the widgets we have; ask before drawing another"). `handoff.mjs::checkHandoff` and Spec 48A change in the same commit so the new files are allowed, and a `components.json` inventory is specified the `METHOD.md` way (countable base, reconciled counts, pointing at elements, re-derived by intake).
2. **A trial export** of a two-page throwaway project under the final instructions, checked with `handoff-check.mjs` and one surface, before either real site is designed (about 15 minutes of Bean's time).
3. **Route fixes from signals already present** (the route session; its prompt needs re-ordering): accordion and carousel recognised by mechanism with the handoff `kind` as cross-check; a heading stays a heading when its children are inline; a pairing rule for one draft element feeding two block parts; one command for a foundation and one for a whole surface including picks and Solve, shared furniture first; the residual Custom CSS writer (FR-48-86 says it is not built); review per surface, with Spec 48 §8 amended; a time-boxed kill line.
4. **Extractor fixes** (this session): accept `translate`, `scale` and `rotate`; an "elements are marked" outcome for reveal; a trace row for every refused record; a gap row for every dropped button look; then re-extract.
5. **Finish the button rule** (FR-32-2): every renderer reads the whole preset, shadow included, behind a per-site switch; variants open-ended, printed from the snapshot's own keys. Fields, notices and cards follow as new namespaces. The larger part of this is after the event unless Bean rules otherwise.
6. **Section-level block choice with a fit grade**, after the trial export shows Claude Design follows the new instructions.
7. **Fresh-site set-up for both sites** (Spec 48 Appendix A) can start while Bean designs.

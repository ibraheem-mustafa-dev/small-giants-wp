## Areas with nothing found

- **Marquee overflow** (`f791e433b`, `cc6f5ca3a`, `b74c498fb`): NOTHING FOUND. `sgs/trust-bar` and `sgs/brand-strip` with `belowBreakpoint: overflow`:
  - A bar that fits stays still at 1440 and 768; one that overflows runs.
  - Padding of 60px per side and a 40px item gap tip it over at the expected widths.
  - A viewport sweep 1440, 900, 600, 400, 320, 500, 800, 1440, 320, 1440 flips both ways with no stuck state.
  - A late font growth (64px injected after load) starts it running; removing the growth stops it.
  - Reduced motion at 375 leaves every bar static with no clones.
  - One logo never runs.
  - Not tested: vertical direction, and an exact-fit boundary to the pixel (the flip widths matched my hand sum within the gap and padding terms).
- **Hidden menus** (`f791e433b`): NOTHING FOUND. On the home page the images inside `sgs-mega-panel` and the nav drawer print `loading="lazy"` and `fetchpriority="auto"` (the Featured and cookie images). The visible hero image takes `high`. See finding 6 for the second `high`.
- **Choice flow very-wide columns** (`4b3c5d95d`): the front end is correct. At 2400px wide: `optionColumnsWide` 4 gives 4 columns and 3 gives 3. At 1440px both stay at 2. A stored 9 clamps to 2 on the front end, and the inspector range is 2 to 4.
- **Fit to screen off** (`4b3c5d95d`): correct on the front end. The class is absent, the cards keep 16:9 (237px at 1440) and the stage keeps its square. The fit guard (a later commit) correctly switches fit off at 1440x560.
- **Resolved values** (`24095ea4f`), front end: container `backgroundColour: var(--wp--preset--color--primary)`, `textColour: rgb(10, 20, 30)`, padding `clamp(1rem, 4vw, 3rem)` (on `__inner`), gap `calc(1rem + 2px)`, radius `clamp(4px, 2vw, 30px)` and min-height `min(50vh, 300px)` all print unchanged and compute correctly at 375, 768 and 1440. A heading `rgb(200,100,50)` background prints `#C86432` on its `::after`. PHP injection probes (`;`, `}`, `url(`, unbalanced, trailing text) are all refused. The editor was checked through `src/utils/tokens.js` in Node only: `clamp`, `calc`, `min` and `var` pass through. `colourVar('rgb(...)')` showed a slug in Node only because `CSS.supports` does not exist there; I did not open the real editor.
- **Type-scale** (`17f2b7115`) on the live page: a heading with `fontSize` set to `clamp(1.5rem, 4vw + 1rem, 4rem)` renders 64, 46.72 and 31px at 1440, 768 and 375 (not stepped down); `5vw` renders 72, 38.4 and 18.75px; `min(8vw, 60px)` renders 60, 60 and 30px. The plain `hero` preset steps 50, 40 and 33px, and `x-large` steps 24, 22 and 21px. All as the message says for block-level sizes. A client `clamp()` preset is restored inside the tablet media query (see finding 4 for what it misses).

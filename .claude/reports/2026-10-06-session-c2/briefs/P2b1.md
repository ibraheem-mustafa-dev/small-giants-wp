# P2b1 - Exclude non-summary children of a closed <details> from layoutElement

## Diagnosis

`gen-help-17` is **correctly paired** - this is not a mispair, it is a descent defect.

`scripts/parity/lib/paint.mjs::layoutElement` descends through single rendered children. The draft removes
the answer when the accordion is closed, so it descends past the wrapper down to the button. Live's closed
`<details>` still has **two** rendered children (the `<summary>` and the collapsed content), so the descent
stops there. The two sides end up measuring different elements.

## The fix

Exclude non-`summary` children of a **closed** `<details>` from the descent, so a closed `<details>`
presents the same single-child shape live as the draft does.

## Owned files

`scripts/parity/lib/paint.mjs`, tests in `scripts/computed-route/tests/walker-refs.test.mjs`.

## Negative control

- **Red on revert:** a case with a closed `<details>` holding a `<summary>` plus collapsed content,
  asserting `layoutElement` descends to the summary/button as the draft does. Reverting must turn it red.
- **Not over-suppressing:** an **open** `<details>` must keep its existing descent behaviour, and a
  `<details>` whose collapsed content is genuinely the only child must not be skipped. The exclusion is
  scoped to closed-and-multiple-children only.

Expected to clear ~5 F rows.

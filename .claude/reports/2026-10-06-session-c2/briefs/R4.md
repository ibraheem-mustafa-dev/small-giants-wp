# R4 - surface the walker's measurement-quality findings in the sweep

## Why this exists

Added 2026-10-06, out of lane W1-C's own report. P3b now emits a **`reveal-unfired`** finding when an
armed entrance never plays, which is the part of that fix that stops it converting a real defect into a
silent clean pass. But `scripts/parity/draft-live-walk.mjs` writes it into `states[ name ].findings`, and
**nothing reads `findings` anywhere in the codebase.** The pre-existing `unsettled` key has no reader
either.

So the walker now knows when its own measurement is untrustworthy and tells nobody. That is the same shape
as R3: a measurement-quality signal that exists but never reaches the person reading the number.

## The fix

Surface both keys in the sweep:

1. Read `states[ * ].findings` and `states[ * ].unsettled` from each surface's walker report.
2. Report them per surface in the sweep output, as a measurement-quality warning distinct from an issue
   row. A `reveal-unfired` means "this surface's resting read may be of an un-played animation", which is a
   caveat on the F count, not an F row itself.
3. Do **not** silently fold them into the issue classes. They are a statement about the instrument, not
   about the client's site, and conflating the two is how a tool loses its own audit trail.

## Owned files

`scripts/computed-route/lib/sweep.mjs`, tests in `scripts/computed-route/tests/sweep.test.mjs`.
Both are already in your lane for R2 and R3 - this is the third task in the same file and the same theme.

**Do not edit `scripts/parity/draft-live-walk.mjs`** to change the emission shape; read what it already
writes. That file is outside your set.

## Negative control

- **Red on revert:** a case with a walker report carrying a `reveal-unfired` finding on one state,
  asserting the sweep reports that surface as having a measurement caveat. Removing the surfacing must turn
  it red.
- **Not over-suppressing, and here it means not over-REPORTING:** a walker report with no `findings` and no
  `unsettled` must produce **no** caveat, and the surface's F count must be unchanged by the presence of a
  caveat. If every surface carries a warning, the warning means nothing and will be ignored - which is
  where `findings` started.

# P2b2 - Retarget the about-step live finder to the step, and delete the stale accept entry

## Diagnosis

**This is a genuine hand-config error** - the only true mispair of the three Session C2 examined.

`sites/eye-care-ward-end/build/qa/parity/home.mjs` targets live
`.sgs-process-steps__step:nth-of-type(i) .sgs-process-steps__title` - **the title, not the step.** The
config's own `accept` note even describes the mispair it created ("The draft's step element contains its
number"), which is the config documenting its own bug rather than fixing it.

Hand pairs are never judged by any gate today, while generated pairs are. That is why this survived.

## The fix

1. Retarget the live finder from `.sgs-process-steps__title` to the **step** itself.
2. **Delete the stale `accept` entry** that papered over the mispair. Leaving it would suppress the rows the
   corrected pairing now measures honestly.

## Why it is sequenced here

**P2a (the twin-containment gate, Wave 2) needs this corrected config as its own positive control.** Do not
leave the config wrong for the gate to flag - fix it, so the gate can be proven against a known-good pair.

## Negative control

- **Red on revert:** a config regression assert that the `about-step-*` live selector resolves to the step
  element and **not** to `.sgs-process-steps__title`. Restoring the old selector must turn it red.
- **Not over-suppressing:** assert the deleted `accept` entry is gone **and** that no other `accept` entry in
  the config was removed with it. Deleting one suppression must not quietly delete its neighbours.

Expected to clear 6 F rows.

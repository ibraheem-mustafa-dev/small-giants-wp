## Recommended pilot: `team-member` — "Card Settings" panel (team-member/edit.js:423)

Reasoning:
- Flat structure: 4 `SelectControl`s + 1 conditionally-shown `ToggleControl` + 1 `ShadowControl` — no nested nested `PanelBody`, no multi-branch conditional rendering to reconcile with ToolsPanel's show/hide-by-default model.
- Not a colour panel — clear of D618/D621 entirely.
- Not duplicated elsewhere (unlike the header/footer-row "Alignment & grid" pair, where converting one without the other would create a visible inconsistency Bean would have to judge twice).
- Every control here is a genuine "optional style adjustment" (heading level, display mode, card style, photo shape, hover overlay, shadow) — a natural fit for ToolsPanel's per-item add/remove/reset semantics, unlike e.g. `nav-drawer`'s "Drawer" panel where several controls are structural/required rather than optional.
- Low blast radius: single content-KIND composite block, no shared-wrapper involvement (per D294, team-member already renders block-private, not through `SGS_Container_Wrapper`), so a pilot conversion cannot regress any other block.

Converting this one block only, then getting Bean's visual sign-off on the resulting "+ " menu / `resetAll` behaviour, is the right gate before touching the other 14 findings — this rule is advisory/informational only and has never gated a build, so there is no urgency pressure to batch it.

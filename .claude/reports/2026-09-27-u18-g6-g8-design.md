## 1. G6 — burger bar size

**Problem.** `.sgs-nav-bar-menu__burger-icon` is a fixed 24x18 box (`nav-bar-menu/style.css`, zero-
specificity defaults, no attribute). The bars span its width and are spaced over its height.

**Design.** Two per-tier length objects on `sgs/nav-bar-menu`: `burgerIconWidth` and `burgerIconHeight`
(`{"type":"object","default":{}}`, empty = today's 24/18). `nav-menu-trigger-css.php` emits
`.{uid} .sgs-nav-bar-menu__burger-icon{width:X;--sgs-nbm-icon-h:Y}` per tier (tier-diffed like
`burgerWidth`), only when set, so an untouched burger (and the two-bar default of 8.5px) is unchanged.
The stylesheet already reads `height:var(--sgs-nbm-icon-h)` and the `line` and two-bar poses derive
their travel from it. The `x` and `x-rotate` poses hard-code `translateY(8px)` (a twin of the 18px
default); they change to `calc((var(--sgs-nbm-icon-h, 18px) - 2px) / 2)`, identical at the default, so
every pose follows the stack height. Controls: "Bar
length" and "Bar stack height" beside Width/Height in the burger panel, on the global device toggle.

## 2. G7 — drawer content at full width

**Problem.** `drawerAlign` (left, center, right) maps to `align-items` on `.sgs-nav-drawer__body`, a
column flexbox, so a container or button row shrinks to its content; nothing can span the drawer.

**Design.** Add `stretch` ("Full width") to the enum and the PHP allow-list in the same commit
(`nav-drawer/render.php` `$allowed_aligns`, `$align_items_map` -> `stretch`, `$text_align_map` -> `start`)
and to `edit.js`'s `ALIGN_ITEMS` map and control. Default stays `left`.

## 3. G8 — a card pinned to a screen corner

**Problem.** Nothing pins an ordinary block to the viewport. The only fixed placements are the
header's own float, `sgs/notice-banner::stickyPosition` and the WhatsApp button.

**Design (recommended A).** `sgs/container` gains `pinToScreen`, a per-tier object whose values are
`off`, `top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-left`, `bottom-right` (default `{}` = off), and `pinInset`, a
per-tier box (`{top,right,bottom,left}`, default 16px on the pinned sides). Rendered through
`SGS_Container_Wrapper` as `position:fixed` plus the two insets for that corner and
`z-index:calc(var(--sgs-header-z,100) + 1)` (the popover layer the drawer's trigger panel uses).
**Printed at the end of `<body>`, not in place:** a blurred or transformed ancestor (any wrapped block
with `surfaceBlur`, `helpers-surface-ground.php` says so) turns `position:fixed` into ancestor-relative,
so a pinned container's HTML is lifted by a `render_block` filter and printed at `wp_footer`, the same
portal the scrim uses (`helpers-scrim.php`). Works without JavaScript. Hiding
at a tier stays the existing device-visibility settings. Editor: a "Pin to a screen corner" control in
the container's layout panel; the editor canvas shows it in place (not fixed) with a small "pinned"
label, so it never covers the canvas.

**Option B (not recommended).** A header "side card" slot: only works inside the header, and lamalama's
card is independent of the pill (different width, top-right while the pill is centred).

**Risk.** `SGS_Container_Wrapper` renders ~40 blocks (hand-back #9 in THE-MIGRATION-METHOD): the new
output is emitted ONLY when `pinToScreen` resolves to a corner on some tier, so every existing container
renders byte-identical CSS (proved by rendering the canary's container fixtures before and after).
A pinned card can cover content: the corner layer sits under the header's own layer, keeps its own
44px targets, and does not trap focus.

identical at the default.

## 7. Two bugs found finishing the Indus panels (causes proven from the code; no design choice)

**G10 — a narrow desktop mega panel stacks its aside below its links.** Every `sgs/mega-panel` root is
an inline-size container (`mega-panel/render.php`, `container-type:inline-size`), and the drawer's
reflow rule is `@container (max-width: 640px){…aside{flex:none;width:100%}}`. A desktop panel of 620px
(Indus About and Trade) is therefore its own "narrow container" and stacks. Fix: emit the
`@container` reflow only when the panel renders inside the drawer (`$sgs_mm_in_drawer`, already known
server-side); the viewport `@media (max-width: 1023px)` reflow is unchanged. Proof: About and Trade's
aside at 300px beside the links at 1440, and still stacked inside the 375 drawer.

**G11 — group-heading rules reach every heading inside a group.** `mega-panel/render.php`
`$heading_sel` and three `mega-panel/style.css` rules use `.sgs-mega-group .wp-block-sgs-heading`
(descendant), so any heading nested deeper in a group takes the group label's style or is visually
hidden. The group's own heading is always a direct child (`mega-group/edit.js` TEMPLATE, printed
straight inside the wrapper by `mega-group/render.php`), so the selectors become `> .wp-block-sgs-heading`.
Proof: a heading nested in a group keeps its own font, size and case; the group label is unchanged.
The Indus copy's row labels then move back from `sgs/text` to `sgs/heading`.

## 8. G8 revision (2026-09-27, implementation objection — plan §5 step 2a)

A read-only census of the portal surface found §3's named mechanism ("lifted by a `render_block`
filter and printed at `wp_footer`, the same portal the scrim uses") does not hold. The approved
BEHAVIOUR is unchanged; the mechanism is revised. Four findings:

**(a) `helpers-scrim.php` is not a `render_block` filter.** `sgs_scrim_render()` is called
synchronously from each adopting block's `render.php`, returns that block's CSS so it stays in flow,
and queues only a CSS-free `aria-hidden` `<div>` through `sgs_scrim_queue()` for
`sgs_scrim_print_footer()`. No framework precedent exists for lifting a block's own content-bearing
markup: every footer portal (`helpers-scrim.php`, `nav-detach-chip.php`,
`class-sgs-drawer-render.php`, `class-sgs-floating-ui-renderer.php`) emits an additional or
decorative element and leaves the block in flow.

**(b) The CSS would be stranded.** `SGS\Blocks\sgs_lift_block_css()`
(`includes/class-sgs-css-registry.php`) runs on `render_block` at priority 99 and extracts each
block's `<style>` into a collected stylesheet that `sgs_css_inject()` writes before `</head>`.
Removing the pinned container's whole output before p99 and printing it at `wp_footer` means the
CSS never reaches the collector: an unstyled pinned card.

**(c) The editor would lose the card.** `wp_footer` does not fire on the block editor's
ServerSideRender/REST route. A footer-only pinned card silently disappears from the canvas. The
scrim can afford that because it is `aria-hidden` decoration; a container's children cannot.

**(d) Two earlier filters are load-bearing for the exit cell.**
`SGS\Blocks\check_conditional_visibility` runs at p9 and can return `''`;
`SGS\Blocks\inject_device_visibility_classes` runs at p10 and adds `sgs-hide-mobile` /
`sgs-hide-tablet` / `sgs-hide-desktop`. A lift earlier than p10 loses the hide classes, which is
exactly how "absent at 375 and 768" was to be delivered.

### Revised mechanism

1. **The CSS path does not change.** `SGS_Container_Wrapper::render()` emits the pin declarations
   (`position:fixed`, the two insets for the corner, `z-index:calc(var(--sgs-header-z, 100) + 1)`)
   into `$responsive_css` as an ordinary `.{uid}{…}` scoped rule, and stamps `data-sgs-pinned="1"`
   on the wrapper. `position`, `z-index` and the insets are real CSS properties, so
   `audit-inline-styling.js` requires exactly this and forbids `$styles` or any inline route.
   A new clause is added to the `$needs_uid` disjunction so a container whose only reason to exist
   is the pin still mints a uid.
2. **Tier hiding moves onto `pinToScreen: off`.** A tier resolving to `off` emits `display:none` in
   the same uid-scoped rule. This drops the dependency on the p10 injector and makes the exit cell
   self-contained rather than contingent on filter order.
3. **The portal is a `render_block` filter at priority 12**, in a new `includes/helpers-pin-portal.php`
   (path confirmed free). It runs only when `SGS\Blocks\sgs_is_frontend_render()`, only for
   `sgs/container`, and only when the output carries `data-sgs-pinned="1"`. It splits the leading
   uid-anchored `<style id="sgs-container-…">…</style>` off the front, queues the remaining markup,
   and returns the style tag alone — so p99's collector still sees the CSS while the markup lands at
   `wp_footer`. Priority 12 sits after p9 (a dropped block leaves nothing to lift) and after the
   p10/p11 injectors (their classes and attributes are already applied).
4. **The `wp_footer` priority is stated, not 5.** Both existing portals register at p5, so their
   relative document order — and therefore z-index tie-breaking — is decided by `require_once` order
   rather than intent. The pin portal registers above both and says so.
5. **Queue semantics are stated.** `sgs_scrim_queue()` never clears on read; the detach chip's does.
   The pin queue keys by uid and does not clear, matching the scrim, documented in its docblock.
6. **Editor:** the filter no-ops off the frontend, so the canvas renders the card in place, unfixed,
   with its "pinned" label — which is what §3 already wanted for the editor.

### Accepted consequences, named

- A pinned container does not receive the `sgsHideOn*` device-visibility classes; `pinToScreen: off`
  is its tier switch instead.
- `--sgs-header-z` is published only by the first `sgs/site-header` of a request that carries an
  authored `zIndex`, per tier inside `@media`. On most pages the custom property does not exist and
  the `, 100` fallback does the work; the pin must not assume it is present or tier-constant.
- This is the **third** `wp_footer` portal consumer and no portal-conformance detector exists
  (`scripts/scrim/check-scrim.py` polices only the scrim). Per `THE-MIGRATION-METHOD.md` a detector
  is owed at the third adopter. Recorded as residue, assigned to the unit row.
- `pinToScreen` is declared on `sgs/container` only. The 39 container-mirror blocks include
  `sgs/site-header`, `sgs/nav-drawer`, `sgs/modal` and `sgs/mega-panel`, for which lifting to the end
  of the body and pinning to a corner would be destructive. Propagation is an operator-reviewed
  attribute diff (`scripts/sync-container-wrapping-blocks.py`), not automatic, so declining it is
  consistent with R-31-9. The wrapper reads the attribute through a `?? array()` guard and the filter
  is gated on the block name, so nothing else can be lifted.

Gates this must satisfy: `audit-inline-styling.js` (no inline paint), `check-box-family-guard.py`
(`pinInset` in `supports.sgs.boxFamilies` and seeded to `box_family`),
`check-editor-render-parity.js` CHECK B (the five-value enum registered in `css-keyword-enums.json`
and validated with `in_array( $raw, $allowed, true )`), `check-element-manifest-conformance.js`,
and STOP-NO-KSORT (the uid is `md5( wp_json_encode( $attributes ) … )` — never sort `$attributes`).
Nothing may be named `*__scrim` and the card must paint no dimmer, or `check-scrim.py` R1/R3 misfire.

Status: awaiting the two-reviewer re-run required by plan §5 step 2a.

## 9. G8 verdict: NO GO — the unit stops here (plan §5 step 2a)

The §8 revision was re-run past two reviewers on 2026-09-27, as plan §5 step 2a requires.

**Census (Haiku, read-only): all 14 factual claims VERIFIED.** The revision's understanding of the
CSS registry, the filter priorities, `sgs_is_frontend_render()`, the wrapper's return shape, the uid
hash, the queue semantics and the gate mechanisms is accurate.

**Adversarial reader (Sonnet, read-only): NO GO.** Two of §8's four load-bearing claims are false
against the code. Both were independently re-verified in the main thread before accepting the
verdict:

1. **The editor claim is unfounded.** §8 step 6 asserts the canvas renders the card "in place,
   unfixed". `SGS_Container_Wrapper` contains zero `is_admin()`, `sgs_is_frontend_render()` or
   `REST_REQUEST` references, so its CSS emission has no editor branch: `position:fixed` and the
   insets reach the canvas unchanged and the card floats over the editing surface inside the iframe
   — precisely what §3 set out to prevent. No precedent solves this either; `sgs/notice-banner`
   applies the same `position:fixed` modifier class in the editor and simply lives with it.
2. **The CSS-collector claim holds only on the happy path.** A pinned container's output can carry
   three or more `<style>` tags, not one: the `fx` filters at p11 insert a second immediately after
   the wrapper's own leading tag, and `render_custom_css` (p10, `return $block_content . $style`)
   and `inject_parallax_attributes` (p11, via `sgs_append_scoped_var_style()`) each append one after
   the wrapper closes. A single uid-anchored front-split therefore strands every one of them in the
   portaled markup, where `sgs_lift_block_css()` never sees them. `fx`, custom CSS and parallax are
   all live, combinable features on `sgs/container`, and a distinctive corner card is exactly the
   kind of block an operator gives them to.

Three further findings, accepted as real and unaddressed by §8:

3. **Nesting inside a drawer or modal is a footgun.** `pinToScreen` resolves at render time with no
   knowledge of open/closed state, and inner blocks render first, so a container placed inside
   `sgs/nav-drawer` to appear only when the drawer opens is instead detached to the footer and
   visible on every page load. The editor would show it correctly nested, so the two surfaces
   disagree completely.
4. **The z-index is coupled to the wrong thing.** Deriving the card's layer from `--sgs-header-z`
   means raising the header's `zIndex` silently lifts the card above `sgs/whatsapp-cta` (200, a fixed
   theme token). And `sgs/notice-banner` in announcement mode is `position:fixed`, full width, `top:0`
   at `z-index:1000` — it occupies exactly the strip a top-right card claims, with no coordination
   designed. The pin needs its own token, not the header's.
5. **§8 undercounts the portal debt.** It says this is the third `wp_footer` portal consumer; there
   are already five (`helpers-scrim.php`, `nav-detach-chip.php`, `class-sgs-drawer-render.php`,
   `class-sgs-floating-ui-renderer.php`, `class-product-item-list.php`), so the pin would be the
   sixth and the portal-conformance detector `THE-MIGRATION-METHOD.md` calls for is long overdue, not
   newly owed.

**Consequence.** One revision was permitted and has been spent, and the re-run says no, so G8 is not
built in this session. G6, G7, G10 and G11 are built and committed independently of it.

Gate 3C item 4 cannot pass while G8 is open: lamalama's "GET IN TOUCH" card (160x326 at top 16 /
right 16 at 1440, absent at 375 and 768) is an exit cell of that item and nothing in the framework can
express it. The other exit cells are unaffected.

**What a future G8 needs**, from the reviewer's own list — this is the spec, not a wish:
(a) consolidate every `<style>` tag found anywhere in the block output, reusing the collector's own
unanchored regex, rather than splitting one off the front; (b) a real editor branch that suppresses
`position:fixed`, the insets and the z-index for the pinned rule, plus the "pinned" label component;
(c) a decision on `pinToScreen` inside `sgs/nav-drawer` and `sgs/modal` — forbid it at authoring time
or document the footgun; (d) the pin's own z-index token, with the notice-banner and WhatsApp-CTA
collisions named; (e) an honest portal count and, at six adopters, the conformance detector.

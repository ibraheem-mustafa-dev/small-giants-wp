# What we can build

This is what the site platform already has, in design words. Everything here can be styled freely: colour, type, spacing, shape, shadow and motion are yours, and so is the layout around and inside each thing. The list is about what a thing is and how it works, not how it looks.

Before drawing a section, widget or control that works differently from everything here, say it would have to be built and ask first.

Any text can carry bold, italic, strikethrough, links and line breaks, a highlighter stripe behind words, and a small photo inside a line. Any box can have a hover lift, an entrance as it scrolls into view, and a looping or scroll-linked effect.

## Sections and layout

**Section or box.** A band or box that holds anything, laid out as a stack, a row or a grid, at full width or at a set content width, with a colour, gradient, photo, video or drawn background.
- It can also: become a row that scrolls sideways, with arrows, dots and drag; lift as one card on hover.

**Hero.** The opening section of a page: a headline, a supporting line and calls to action, over or beside a picture, video or drawn graphic.
- Layout: words over a full background, words beside the picture.
- Words: at the start, centred.

**Floating decoration.** A picture, a word, a label or a badge floated over its section at a set position, size and angle.
- It can also: be mirrored; fade as the page scrolls.

**Divider.** A horizontal line between parts, in any thickness, width, colour or gradient.
- Middle: a plain line, an icon in the middle, a word in the middle.

**Playful section.** A section whose decorative items can be picked up and thrown about.

## Text, pictures and small parts

**Heading.** A heading at any of six levels, or a sub-heading line.
- Kind: heading, sub-heading.

**Text.** A paragraph of body text.
- It can also: open with a large first letter; fold behind a Read more and Read less control.

**Label, eyebrow or badge.** A short kicker above a heading, a pill, a tag or a badge.
- Marker beside the words: a dot, a short line, an icon.
- Shaped ends: arrow, triangle, slant, curve, wave, zigzag.

**Quote.** A pulled quote of one or more paragraphs.
- It can also: name who said it.

**Picture, video or drawn graphic.** One photo, video, embedded player or drawn graphic, with a different file for phone, tablet and desktop where the design needs one. It can be cropped to a shape, framed, captioned and linked.

**Gallery.** Several pictures as a grid, a staggered wall or a carousel.
- It can also: open a picture large over the page; show captions; play by itself, with arrows, dots and a pause control.

**Before and after.** Two pictures or videos compared by dragging a divider across them.
- Divider moves: side to side, up and down.
- It can also: label each side.

**Icon.** An icon, an emoji or a drawn mark, bare or inside a shaped background, with an optional label.
- Background shape: square, circle, pill, hexagon, diamond, octagon, star, blob.
- Label: after, before, below, above.

**Button and button row.** A button or link in the versions your Button component declares, alone or in a row that stacks or wraps on small screens.
- Icon: before the words, after the words, alone, with no words showing.

**Animated number.** A number that counts up when it comes into view, with a prefix, a suffix and a label.

**Countdown.** A countdown to a date, or one that starts fresh for each visitor.
- Digits: plain, flipping cards.

**Audio player.** A sound player.
- Look: a quiet pill, a waveform, live spectrum bars, a radial ring, an oscilloscope line, a pulsing gradient, no visible player, a sound on and off button.

**Measured drawing.** A drawing with labelled measurement lines over it.
- Labels: written on the drawing, numbered, with a key beside it.

**Scroll-played picture sequence.** A run of frames that plays forwards and backwards as the page scrolls, like a product turning.

**Contents list.** A list of the page's headings that jumps to each and marks where the reader is.
- List: numbered, bulleted, plain.

**Live clock, dark mode switch, language and country links.** Small working parts: a clock for one time zone, a light and dark switch, a list of language links, and a country or store chooser.

## Things that open, switch or move

**Accordion.** Rows that open to show an answer: FAQs, product details, policies. Each answer can hold anything.
- Look: boxed rows, rows divided by lines, separate cards.
- Open and close icon: at the end, at the start.
- It can also: keep several rows open, or one at a time; be narrowed by a search box; be narrowed by topic chips; show a topic above each question.

**Tabs.** A row or column of tabs, one panel showing at a time. Each panel can hold anything.
- Tabs run: across the top, down the side.
- Look: underlined, boxed, pills.
- On a phone: tabs stack, tabs stay in a row that scrolls.

**Review or quote slider.** Customer reviews or quotes as a slider, or a single one standing alone. Each holds the quote, a name, a role and organisation, a rating, a photo, a logo and a work picture, each optional.
- Card: a classic card, a large editorial quote, led by the rating, a review card, led by the person's photo, led by the organisation's logo, with a case-study picture, the quote alone.
- Change: slides across, fades up.
- Rating: stars, a score out of a number.
- It can also: play by itself, with a pause control; show arrows; show dots.

**Timeline.** Dated or numbered moments along a line.
- Entries sit: on alternate sides, all on one side, in one column.
- As the page scrolls: entries appear, the line fills with the scroll, the section holds still while the entries pass, the section holds still and travels sideways.
- On a phone: stacked, a swipeable row.

**Numbered steps.** The steps of a process in a row, each with a number, a title and a line of text.
- Joined by: a line, arrows, dots.
- Number: in a circle, in a square, not shown.

**Pop-up.** A panel that opens over the page from a button or link and holds anything. It closes with its close control, the Escape key or a click outside.
- Width: small, medium, large, full width.
- Size: a box, the whole screen.

**Notice or announcement strip.** A message in the page, a bar across it, or an announcement strip above the header.
- Place: in the page, a bar across the page, the strip above the header.
- Tone: information, success, warning, error, brand accent.
- Messages: one message, several, shown in turn, several, one picked at random.
- It can also: be closed by the visitor.

**Choice chips.** A set of pills or tiles where the visitor picks one: a pack size, a flavour, a colour, a plan.
- Look: outlined, filled, bare until chosen, tiles.
- Size: small, medium, large.

**Step-by-step chooser.** A run of questions where each answer can lead to a different next question, ending in a recommendation, an add to basket or an email. It can show a running price and summary.
- Layout: every question visible in the page, one question at a time, large.
- Progress: a bar, circles, a step count.
- Ends with: a recommendation, add to basket, an email.

**Live message preview.** Text boxes whose typing appears live elsewhere on the page, such as a gift note on a card.

## Cards, lists and collections

**Card grid.** A grid of cards, each with a picture, a title, text and a link.
- Card: words below the picture, words laid over the picture.
- Filled from: cards written by hand, the latest posts or pages, shop products, a collection of the site's own items.
- More: no more, numbered pages, a Load more button, loads as the page scrolls.

**Feature cards.** Benefit or feature cards, each with an icon or picture, a title, a sub-title, text and a button, in any order.
- Icon or picture: above the words, at the start, at the end.
- Grid: cards wrap to fit the width, a set number of columns.

**List.** A list of lines or links, with an optional heading.
- Marker: an icon per line, an emoji per line, bullets, numbers, none.
- It cannot do: Letters or Roman numerals, a list that starts from a chosen number or counts down, and items laid out across columns row by row.

**Blog and article grid.** Posts as a grid, a list, a staggered wall or a carousel, each with a picture, title, extract, date, author and category, each optional.
- Card: a card, flat, words over the picture, words only.
- More: no more, numbered pages, a Load more button, loads as the page scrolls.
- It can also: be filtered by category or tag.

**Pricing table.** Plans side by side, each with a price, a feature list, a button and an optional badge.
- Billing switch: none, monthly and yearly, monthly only, yearly only.

**Team member.** A person's card: photo, name, role, a short biography and social links.
- Size: full, compact.

## Reviews, trust and contact

**Trust strip.** A full-width strip of short trust points, such as accreditations and delivery promises. It can sit still or scroll as a ticker.
- Each point: an icon in a circle, a bare icon, words only, a picture badge.
- Size: small, medium, large.

**Logo strip.** A row of brand or partner logos, still or scrolling for ever, with faded edges.
- Logos: in tiles, in a plain row.
- Shows: logos, names, a logo, or the name where there is none.

**Google and Trustpilot reviews.** Reviews pulled from Google or Trustpilot, and a compact Google rating badge. These keep the platform's own look, so draw them only where the design means to show that platform; reviews in the client's own style are the review slider.
- Google layout: grid, slider, list, wall.
- Trustpilot layout: carousel, grid, list, a small summary, a small carousel.
- Badge: pill, card, stacked.

**Star rating.** Stars, with half stars.
- Shows: stars, stars and the score, stars, the score and the number of reviews.

**Business details.** The business's phone, email, address, opening hours, an open-now line and a map, kept in one place and shown wherever they are drawn.
- Hours: a row per day, days with the same hours grouped.
- Map tint: none, grey, muted, sepia, high contrast, dark.

**Social and contact icons.** A row of icons linking to the business's phone, email, address and social profiles.
- Colour: the surrounding text colour, the site's colours, each network's own colour as the background, each network's own colour on the icon.

**WhatsApp button.** A way to start a WhatsApp chat.
- Form: a button in the page, a button floating in a corner, a banner, a card.

## Forms

**Form.** A form in one page or several steps, with a sent message, error messages and emails to the owner and the sender. Fields: short text, email, phone, number, date, long text with a character count, dropdown, a pick-one group, a tick-box group, a consent tick, picture tiles, file upload and address.
- Field width: full, half, a third, as wide as its content.
- Label: above the box, inside the box, moving up when typed in.
- Tick-box options: plain, cards.
- A chosen tile shows: a tick, a border, a fill.
- Upload area: a dashed drop area, a panel, the browser's own button.
- Step markers: stacked, in a line.
- It can also: show a step count; keep a half-filled form for the visitor's return; end with a review of the answers, each with an Edit link.

## Header, footer and navigation

**Header.** The site header in up to three rows: a strip above, the main bar, and a strip below. It can stick, shrink as the page scrolls, blur what passes behind it and sit transparent over the first section.

**Navigation bar and mega menus.** A row of links that becomes a burger below a chosen width. An item can open a simple drop-down or a wide panel of link columns, cards or brands with a featured side panel.
- Sub-menus open on: hover, click.
- Panel: columns of links, cards, minimal.
- Side panel: a feature, a preview, a call to action.

**Menu drawer.** The panel the burger opens, holding the menu and anything else: a logo, a search, contact icons, a rating, buttons.
- Opens: over the whole screen, below the header, from the start side, from the end side, inside its section, from the burger itself, as a centred box, as a box under the header.
- Sub-menus: open in place, slide to a new level.

**Footer.** The site footer in up to three rows: a strip above, a grid of up to six columns, and a bottom bar.

**Logo.** The logo, with a different file for desktop, tablet and phone where the design needs one, and an optional drawn-on animation.

**Breadcrumbs.** The trail of pages above the current one, made from the site's own structure.

**Search.** A search box that suggests products as the visitor types.
- Form: a bar in the page or header, an icon that opens into a box, an icon that opens a full-screen search, a quick-find box opened from the keyboard.

## Shop

**Product card.** A product in a grid or carousel: photo, name, price, rating, badges, option pills and one or two buttons.
- Main button: goes to the product, adds to the basket, goes straight to checkout.

**Buy box.** The buying area of a product page: gallery, name, price, option choices, quantity and add to basket.
- Option choices: outlined pills, filled pills, bare until chosen, tiles.
- It can also: keep an add-to-basket bar in view as the page scrolls; show stock; offer a tell-me-when-it-is-back form on a sold-out product.

**Basket.** The header's basket control with its live count, opening a small panel or a drawer of the basket's lines. With it: a free-delivery progress bar, a gift switch with a recipient and note, a line of small print with a link, and a pay-later message.
- Opens: the basket page, a small panel under the control, a drawer.
- Control: an icon, a pill with words.
- Quantity: a number box, a minus and plus stepper.

**Account.** Sign in, create account and lost password, then the account: a dashboard, orders, one order with its items, addresses, account details and saved items.
- Sign in and create account: side by side, stacked, as two tabs.
- Account menu: a side menu, tabs.

**Saved items.** A heart control with a live count, and the list of saved products with move to basket, tell me when it is back, and remove.

**Product questions.** Questions and answers for a product page, as rows that open.

**Filter search.** A type-to-find box that narrows a long list of filter choices, such as brands.
